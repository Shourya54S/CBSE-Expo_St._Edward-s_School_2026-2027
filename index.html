Resilience in everyday life.

Indu Devi, a domestic worker in Shimla, rebuilt her family’s life after her husband left behind debt and no steady income.

Born without fingers on her right hand, she worked in several homes, made paper bags with old newspapers, repaid the full loan and continued supporting the education of all four children.
